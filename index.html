About LinaBell

LinaBell is a pink fox from Duffy and Friends. She debuted at Shanghai Disney Resort in 2021 and is known for her curiosity, intelligence and detective skills. Together with her magnifying glass, she loves exploring nature and solving mysteries.

Personality Traits

🔍 Inquisitive & Smart

LinaBell loves finding clues and solving mysteries.

🦊 Detective Spirit

She uses observation and reasoning to help her friends.

🌿 Nature Lover

LinaBell enjoys spending time outdoors and learning about nature.

✨ Fun Loving

She is playful, energetic and brings joy to everyone around her.
